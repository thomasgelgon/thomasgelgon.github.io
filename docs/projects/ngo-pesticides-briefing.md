# NGO Campaign Briefing to Ban Urban Pesticides in the UK

![Project overview image](../assets/images/project3-cover.png)

## Overview

Policy briefing written for an environmental policy course, presenting a coalition campaign strategy to secure a national ban on urban pesticides in the UK, addressing the health and environmental risks of urban pesticide use and the political obstacles to regulation.

**Study Area:** United Kingdom
**Duration:** April 2025
**Role:** Solo project
**Status:** Completed

---

## Methods & Tools

**Data Sources**

- Peer-reviewed literature on pesticide health and environmental impacts
- DEFRA's 2020 pesticides consultation and national action plan
- Pesticide Action Network (PAN) UK data on existing local pesticide bans

**Processing Steps**

1. Literature review on urban pesticide health and environmental impacts
2. Analysis of the UK political and regulatory landscape (DEFRA inaction, fragmented local bans)
3. Identification of stakeholders and coalition partners (ENGOs, health NGOs, existing pesticide networks)
4. Mapping of political obstacles (industry lobbying, public perception, governmental constraints) and counter-strategies
5. Drafting of campaign approach and briefing document

---

## Key Findings

- Pesticide exposure is linked to significant health risks, including cancer, diabetes, Parkinson's disease and asthma, and causes an estimated 200,000 deaths worldwide per year
- Only around 0.1% of applied pesticides reach their intended target, with the remainder contaminating soil, water, air and surrounding urban areas
- Despite DEFRA's 2020 consultation, the UK still has no national ban on urban pesticides, only a fragmented patchwork of local council bans — highlighting the need for coordinated coalition pressure combining public campaigning, MP lobbying and scientific credibility
